How it works

  1. 1
    Your words are parsed

    Budget, category, brand and intent are extracted — "under ₦200k" becomes a real price ceiling.

  2. 2
    The catalogue is searched

    TF-IDF retrieval ranks every product, then description filters narrow it down.

  3. 3
    Only real products come back

    Products always come from retrieval. A language model, when configured, only writes the surrounding prose — it cannot invent a product or a price.
